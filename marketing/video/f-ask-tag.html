Glycolic Exfoliating Toner
How often should I use this?

Your Glycolic Exfoliating Toner has glycolic acid high up its ingredient list, so it’s a real exfoliant, not just a toner.

2–3 evenings a week is plenty for most skin. Keep it off the nights you use your Retinol 0.3% Night Serum: alternate them.

If your skin feels tight or stings, drop to once a week.
