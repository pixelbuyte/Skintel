## Posting notes
- **Sound:** every cut is silent by design so you can drop any trending audio on top without
  fighting a voiceover. If you'd rather have VO, the scripts in `VOICEOVER.md` are timed to the
  scene boundaries.
- **First frame matters** — TikTok/IG use it as the cover. V1 opens on the hook line, V3 opens
  on "500", Versus opens on "$48. Or $12." All work as static covers.
- **Sequence for a launch week:** Hero (all-rounder) → Versus (widest reach) → Shelf (utility) →
  Routine (tips audience) → V3 or Progress to the people who watched any of them.
- **Post one a day, not all at once.** Five videos dropped in an afternoon compete with each
  other for the same audience and the algorithm splits reach between them. Spaced out, each one
  gets its own test.
- **Keep the seat count honest.** The videos say "500 spots" without claiming how many are left.
  If you want live scarcity ("only 80 left"), say it in the caption where you can update it —
  not burned into the video.
- **The numbers in these videos are illustrations, not customer data.** Fragranced lotion at 31,
  the 76% drop, the twelve-week curve — all worked examples built to show what the app does.
  That's normal for product demos and completely fine, right up until a caption implies a real
  person's results. Keep the captions describing the *product*, not a testimonial you don't have.
