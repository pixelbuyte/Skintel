When should I use my retinol?

At night. Your Retinol 0.3% Night Serum is already in your night routine, right after your toner. Keep it there.

Start with 2–3 nights a week and build up as your skin settles. Skip the Glycolic Exfoliating Toner on retinol nights.

Wear Daily Mineral SPF 50 every morning while you’re using it.
