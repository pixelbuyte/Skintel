# Skintel — 3 Production-Ready Scripts

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 30–35 sec each
**Style:** native phone footage, real bathroom, no editing polish, auto-captions only
**Rule:** say "Skintel" out loud exactly once, plus a 2-second text overlay. No "link in bio."

Each script has a shot list, on-screen text, and a clean voiceover block. **For ElevenLabs, paste only the text inside the code block** — nothing else.

---

## Script 1 — "Three products, one ingredient"

The strongest of the three. Surprising, instantly checkable against the viewer's own shelf, and it's the core product insight.

### Shots

| Time | What to film |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Three products already lined up on the counter. Start talking before the shot settles — no intro. |
| 0:02–0:08 | Pick each one up as you name it. Handheld, let it be slightly shaky. |
| 0:08–0:20 | **Screen recording**: scan product 1 → verdict. Scan product 2 → verdict. Scan product 3 → verdict. Cut between them fast. |
| 0:20–0:28 | Screen recording of the trigger map / the shared ingredient highlighted. **Hold 2 full seconds in silence.** |
| 0:28–0:33 | Back to your face or the three products. Say the name. |

### On-screen text
- 0:00 — `3 PRODUCTS. SAME INGREDIENT.`
- 0:12 — `ISOPROPYL MYRISTATE` (as it appears in the scan)
- 0:20 — `COMEDOGENIC 4/5`
- 0:31 — `SKINTEL` (hold 2 sec)

### Voiceover

```
Three products. Different brands. Different prices.
All three have the same pore-clogging ingredient in them.
Isopropyl myristate. Comedogenic rating four out of five.
It's in the moisturizer, it's in the sunscreen, and it's in the primer you use every single day.
So you're not reacting to one bad product. You're getting hit with the same thing three times before you leave the house.
That's what this finds. Skintel.
```

## Script 2 — "Clean is a marketing word"

Highest comment-driver of the three. People argue about this one, and arguments are reach.

### Shots

| Time | What to film |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Hold up a product that says clean / gentle / natural on the front. Cover the brand name with your thumb. |
| 0:03–0:06 | Flip it around to the ingredient list. Slow push in. |
| 0:06–0:16 | **Screen recording**: scan it, let the flags come up one by one. |
| 0:16–0:26 | Zoom on the flagged sensitizers in the verdict. |
| 0:26–0:32 | Back to the bottle, front label. Say the name. |

### On-screen text
- 0:00 — `IT SAYS "CLEAN"`
- 0:08 — `LINALOOL · LIMONENE · GERANIOL`
- 0:18 — `ALL FRAGRANCE ALLERGENS`
- 0:30 — `SKINTEL` (hold 2 sec)

### Voiceover

```
This says clean. Says gentle. Says dermatologist tested.
Turn it around.
Linalool. Limonene. Geraniol.
Those are three of the most common fragrance allergens there are, and they never have to write the word fragrance on the front.
Clean is a marketing word. The ingredient list is the actual answer.
Skintel reads it, so you don't have to know what any of that means.
```

## Script 3 — "Why cutting products didn't work"

The empathy one. Speaks directly to someone who's already tried everything — the person most likely to actually pay.

### Shots

| Time | What to film |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | Your full shelf or a drawer of products. Wide, messy, real. |
| 0:04–0:10 | Pull three products out and set them aside like you're eliminating them. |
| 0:10–0:22 | **Screen recording**: scan through several fast, verdicts stacking. |
| 0:22–0:30 | The personal trigger map screen. **Hold in silence 2 sec.** |
| 0:30–0:34 | Face to camera. Say the name. |

### On-screen text
- 0:00 — `CUT ACTIVES ✓ FRAGRANCE FREE ✓ STILL BREAKING OUT`
- 0:14 — `IT'S NOT THE PRODUCT`
- 0:22 — `IT'S ONE INGREDIENT. IN ALL OF THEM.`
- 0:32 — `SKINTEL` (hold 2 sec)

### Voiceover

```
You cut out actives. You went fragrance free. You simplified down to three products.
And you're still breaking out.
Here's why that doesn't work.
You're guessing at products, when the thing causing it is one ingredient hiding across all of them.
Cut the product, the ingredient's still in the next one.
This tracks every ingredient in everything you own, and tells you which one keeps showing up when your skin gets worse.
Stop guessing at products. Find the ingredient. Skintel.
```

## ElevenLabs settings

- **Voice:** natural and conversational. Not a narrator, not an announcer. If it sounds like an ad read, pick a different voice.
- **Stability:** 40–50 (lower = more natural variation)
- **Similarity:** high
- **Delivery:** slightly fast, like explaining something to a friend
- Generate once. Don't regenerate chasing perfection — the slightly-imperfect take performs better.
