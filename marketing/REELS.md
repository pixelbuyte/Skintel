## The funnel (every reel points here)

1. Viewer comments **SKINSTEL**.
2. Auto-DM: "Follow @skins_tel and reply DONE and I'll send it over."
3. Second DM: **skinstel.com**.

Instagram doesn't send keyword DMs by itself: it needs ManyChat (or a similar tool) connected to
@skins_tel, with SKINSTEL as the trigger word. Set this up **before** the first funnel reel goes
live, otherwise the comments pile up with no reply.

Say the keyword in three places on every funnel reel: **on screen** at the end, **in the caption**
first line, and **in a pinned comment** ("comment SKINSTEL and I'll DM you the link 💌").

## Rules for every reel

- **Demo first, never lecture first.** Show the shelf, the scan, the result. Talk over it if you like.
- **Hook in 0–3 seconds**, no "hey guys", no logo first.
- **No medical claims.** No "cures acne", no "treats", no diagnosis words. Say "ingredient you
  keep reacting to", "pattern", "conflict", "too much at once". Skintel finds patterns; it doesn't
  diagnose.
- **Look:** cream + terracotta, warm light, Instrument Serif for big words, DM Sans for small ones.
  The teardrop mascot is the recurring character.
- **Our edge vs SkinSort:** they score one product at a time. We check **your whole routine for
  conflicts** and find **what your bad days have in common**. Say that, not "AI ingredient analysis".

## Reel 2 — Recreate-a-format, with credit (reach)

**Job:** reach and goodwill. Turn a trending skincare-education format into a visual demo.

**Hook:** split screen. Top: "their version" (a talking-head skincare tip, credited). Bottom: "our
Skintel version" (the same tip shown with the shelf and a scan).

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Split screen: their clip (credited) / your clip, each labelled with a date | "their version · Oct 3" / "the Skintel version · Oct 24" |
| 2–12s | Your half plays: the tip shown with real products + the scan result | the tip in 6 words max |
| 12–15s | You to camera | "recreate this with YOUR routine and tag @skins_tel" |

**Longer play — our own signature format, the "shelf audit":** line up your whole routine, scan each
product, count the conflicts out loud: "…and that's THREE." Invite people to recreate it and tag
you. Every recreation is free reach that credits back to Skintel.

**Caption:**
> boring skincare lectures are over, show me the shelf 🧴 recreate this with your routine and tag
> @skins_tel, I'll repost my favourites. Comment SKINSTEL if you want the link to scan yours 💌

## Check before posting

- **"Skintel" vs "Skinstel".** The app, the App Store slides and the videos say **Skintel**. The
  keyword, the handle (@skins_tel) and the domain use **Skinstel**. People will see both. Pick one
  spelling for the keyword going forward, or say it on screen once: "Skintel (skinstel.com)".
- **"Free conflict checker".** The brief uses that phrase, but routine conflict checks are a
  **Skintel+** feature in the app. The videos say "I'll DM you the link" / "scan your own shelf for
  free" instead: the download and the free tier are free, the conflict check is not.
- **Founding offer.** skinstel.com describes it as for people who join *before* the iOS app ships.
  The app is live now. Confirm the 500 × $20 offer is still open before posting Reel 8 or the CTA
  clip, or swap the last line of `reel-cta.html`.
- **Instagram links.** I couldn't open the reference reels from here (Instagram blocks it). These
  adaptations follow the written brief.

## Re-rendering the videos

From `marketing/video/` (Node 20+, ffmpeg):

```bash
npm ci
python3 ../app-store/render.py              # also exports the app screens the reels use
node render.mjs reel-droste 30              # → out/skintel-reel-droste.mp4
node render.mjs reel-fights 30
node render.mjs reel-cta 30
node shoot-overlays.mjs                     # → out/overlays/*.png
node render-alpha.mjs plane 1               # → out/plane-f1.mov/.webm/-preview.mp4/-print.png (also 2, 3)
npm run render:features                     # → out/skintel-f-*.mp4 (the six feature videos)
```

Set `CHROME_PATH` to a Chromium binary if Playwright didn't install its own. Copy lives in the
`.html` files; change a word there and re-run.
