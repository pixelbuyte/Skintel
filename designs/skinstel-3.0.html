In the app now

Launch bloomPushed

The drop falls with gravity, squashes on landing with a light haptic, three rings spread, “Skinstel” rises out of a blur, then the iris reveals the app. About 1.6 s on a cold launch. It never takes touches, so the screen underneath works straight away. SKBloomIntro AppRouter.swift

Today unfoldsPushed

The greeting and each card rise out of a soft blur, 70 ms apart, on a spring. .skEntrance(_:) HomeView.swift

Scroll depthPushed

Cards shrink a little, fade and soften as they cross the top or bottom edge, and settle as they arrive. .skScrollDepth()

Warm auroraPushed

Four soft glows in clay, peach, sand and sage drift on slow, out-of-step loops behind the Today header. Drawn on a 30 fps canvas. SKAurora

Springy tabsPushed

The selected tab icon springs up 12%, on both the classic and the iOS 26 glass tab bar. MainTabView.swift

Verdict ring landsRound 2

A bright bead rides the arc while the score counts up, then the ring pops from 94% and glows once. On the verdict screen a medium haptic lands with it; Compare stays quiet because several rings land together. SKScoreRing VerdictView.swift

Mood chips reactRound 2

The picked chip swells and its dot grows, the others step back to 96% and fade. The streak is now one label, so its number rolls when today’s log extends the run. MoodChoiceButton HomeView.swift

Insights draw inRound 2

Header and cards unfold like Today, the week’s dots pop in left to right, and progress bars fill from the left the first time they appear, wherever the app uses them. .skPopIn(_:) SKProgressBar JournalView.swift

Reduce MotionRespected

With Reduce Motion on, all of the above switch off: no launch sequence, no bead or pop, chips and dots appear in place, the aurora holds still.
