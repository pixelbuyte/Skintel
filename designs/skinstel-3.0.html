▶ Playing the tour · touch the phone to take over

Everything in the phone works: tap a mood, the + button, the tabs, the Ask card. Sample data.

Motion pass · round 4 · playable

Skinstel 3.0

The phone on the left is the app with every new motion in it, running live. Open it and a clay drop falls into rings while the name rises through them. Scan a bottle and the brackets lock onto the barcode before the verdict lands with a glow. Ask types its answer as it thinks. The membership card is holographic and leans as you move.

Try it

Touch the water

Tap anywhere

The ripple shader that ships in the app, running here in WebGL on a still of Today. Each tap drops a ring that travels out at 800 points a second, bends the screen under it and catches light on the crest.

In the app it fires three times: through the launch screen when the drop lands, across the skin-log card from your finger when you pick a mood, and across the verdict card from the ring when the score lands.

In the app now

Launch bloomRound 1

The drop falls, lands with a haptic, three rings spread, the name rises out of a blur, and an iris opens onto the app in about 1.6 s. It never blocks touches. SKBloomIntro

Today, Insights, Verdict and Shelf unfoldRounds 1–3

Sections rise out of a soft blur one after another, cards lean back at the scroll edges, and a warm aurora drifts behind Today. .skEntrance .skScrollDepth SKAurora

Verdict ring landsRound 2

A bead rides the arc while the score counts up, then the ring pops, glows once and plays a haptic. SKScoreRing

Mood chips react, streak rollsRound 2

The pick swells, the rest step back, and the streak number rolls. MoodChoiceButton

Water ripplesRound 3

A Metal shader bends the screen in a travelling ring on launch, on a mood pick and when a verdict lands. SKRipple.metal

Holographic membership cardRound 4

The card on You leans a few degrees with the phone, a light band slides across it and a warm iridescence turns with the tilt. It reads the phone’s motion sensor only while the card is on screen; no permission prompt. SKHolo.swift

Shelf zooms into productsRound 4

On iOS 18 and later, a product’s page grows out of its row and shrinks back into it. iOS 17 keeps the normal push. .skZoomSource
