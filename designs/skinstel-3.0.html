Skinstel 3.0

Opening the app now has a moment. A clay drop falls and lands in rings, the name rises letter by letter, and an iris opens from the drop onto Today. Today unfolds section by section under a slow warm light, and the cards lean back as they scroll away.

In the app now

Launch bloomPushed

The drop falls with gravity, squashes on landing with a light haptic, three rings spread, “Skinstel” rises out of a blur, then the iris reveals the app. About 1.6 s on a cold launch. It never takes touches, so the screen underneath works straight away. SKBloomIntro AppRouter.swift

Today unfoldsPushed

The greeting and each card rise out of a soft blur, 70 ms apart, on a spring. .skEntrance(_:) HomeView.swift

Scroll depthPushed

Cards shrink a little, fade and soften as they cross the top or bottom edge, and settle as they arrive. .skScrollDepth()

Warm auroraPushed

Four soft glows in clay, peach, sand and sage drift on slow, out-of-step loops behind the Today header. Drawn on a 30 fps canvas. SKAurora

Springy tabsPushed

The selected tab icon springs up 12%, on both the classic and the iOS 26 glass tab bar. MainTabView.swift

Reduce MotionRespected

With Reduce Motion on, all of the above switch off: no launch sequence, cards appear in place, the aurora holds still.
